Privacy Policy

Dream Seller (the “App”)
Effective date: October 13, 2026

Exit404 (the “Developer”) respects the privacy of everyone who plays the App. This page explains what information is involved and how it is handled.

1. The Developer collects nothing directly

The App has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, or any similar detail. The Developer does not run any server that collects or stores information that could identify you.

The name you choose in-game and your progress (money, day, items you hold, and so on) are stored only on your own device and are never sent anywhere. Deleting the App removes that saved data along with it.

2. Information collected through advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads. Ads are optional rewards that you choose to watch, and the game can be played all the way through without watching any.

While serving ads, Google and its partners may collect or use the following information. None of it is passed to the Developer, who neither views nor stores it.

ItemDescription
Advertising identifierThe identifier your device assigns for advertising (IDFA on iOS, for example)
IP addressMay be used to determine an approximate location
Device informationDevice model, operating system version, language settings, and similar details
Ad interactionsHow often an ad was shown, whether it was tapped, and so on
DiagnosticsTechnical records relating to errors and performance

You can read how Google handles this information here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

3. Tracking permission (iOS)

On iOS, a system prompt asking whether you allow tracking appears once, the first time you play. This prompt is required by Apple, and the choice is entirely yours.

  • If you allow it, the ads you see may be matched to your interests.
  • If you decline, every feature of the game remains available, including the ad rewards. The ads simply will not be matched to your interests.

You can change this choice at any time in your device settings.
Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking

4. Resetting your advertising identifier or turning off personalized ads

  • iOS — Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking lets you turn tracking off per app, and Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising lets you turn off personalized ads.
  • Android — Settings › Google › Ads lets you delete or reset your advertising ID.

5. Children under 13

The App is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in the Republic of Korea), and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe such information has been collected, please contact us at the address below and we will look into it.

6. Retention and deletion

Because the Developer holds no user information, there is no retention period and no deletion procedure to follow. Game data saved on your device is removed when you delete the App. Information held by Google in the course of serving ads is governed by the Google Privacy Policy linked in section 2.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your information. However, because the Developer holds no information that could identify you, we are unable to locate and act on the records of any particular person. Advertising-related information can be managed directly using the steps in sections 3 and 4. If you have any questions, please contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy is revised because of a change in the law or in the App itself, the updated text will be posted on this page together with its effective date. Significant changes will also be announced within the App.
